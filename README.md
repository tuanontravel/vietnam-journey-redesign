Designed Journeys Partner Portal

GOAL

Redesign the existing Vietnam Private Journey landing page using the exact visual design language of Designed Journeys Partner Portal.

IMPORTANT:

The existing Absolute Asia Travel page is the CONTENT SOURCE.

Designed Journeys Partner Portal is the DESIGN SOURCE.

Do not mix their content.

Keep Absolute Asia Travel branding.

Do not redesign this as a B2B website.

TARGET AUDIENCE

Discerning international travelers looking for private tailor made Vietnam journeys, especially:

- couples

- mature travelers

- travelers 55+

- families

- multigenerational families

- higher value private travelers

The design should feel:

calm

refined

editorial

personal

trustworthy

premium

authentic

human

Avoid the look of an OTA, package tour website or aggressive sales funnel.

- all important CTAs

==================================================

Retain the main proposition:

Keep:

- CTA

ITINERARY

==================================================

Keep ALL 10 days and all meaningful details.

Create an elegant itinerary using:

Mobile:

accordion

Hoi An

Keep hotel, meal, activity, transfer and flight information from the source.

==================================================

VIDEOS

==================================================

Keep ALL source traveler videos.

Use thumbnail plus click to load instead of loading every YouTube iframe immediately.

Create:

- one featured video

- traveler video gallery

- clear traveler name / context

==================================================

REVIEWS

==================================================

Preserve all meaningful reviews and current review segmentation.

Possible filters:

All Reviews

Travelers 55+

Couples & Friends

Multigenerational Families

Make testimonials feel authentic and editorial, not like generic cards.

Keep TripAdvisor links where available.

==================================================

LEAD MAGNET

==================================================

Add a secondary conversion path for visitors who are interested but not ready to request a custom trip.

Create a premium lead magnet section:

FREE VIETNAM PRIVATE TRAVEL PLANNING GUIDE

Suggested headline:

Planning Vietnam?

Start With Our Private Travel Guide.

Supporting copy:

Get our practical guide to planning a private Vietnam journey, including route ideas, best travel periods, hotel guidance, pacing advice and tips from our Hanoi based travel team.

Form fields:

First Name

Email

CTA:

Get My Free Vietnam Guide

After submission:

show a simple thank you state and offer:

Ready to go further?

Customize My Vietnam Journey

The lead magnet must remain SECONDARY to the primary enquiry CTA.

Do not use aggressive popups.

Optionally show a subtle lead magnet invitation after the visitor reaches approximately 35 to 50% scroll depth.

==================================================

==================================================

Make the founders section human and editorial.

Use real portraits and existing source information.

Preserve:

- founder story

- team

- Hanoi base

- tour operator license

- license number

- official license image

- company contact details

Make the license image viewable at larger size.
